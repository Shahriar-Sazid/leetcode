# DSA Study Plan — 16 Weeks (4 Months), 30 min × 3 days/week

Week-by-week plan of the most common interview problems. Every week mixes
Easy / Medium / Hard so you keep building confidence, pattern recognition,
and stretch skills in parallel — not Easy-only months, then Medium-only,
then Hard-only.

## Time budget by difficulty

| Difficulty | Sessions | How it's spent |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 1 session (30 min) | Solve fully. If done early, re-explain the pattern out loud or try an alternate approach — don't start a new problem mid-slot. |
| Medium | 1 session (30 min) | Timer on. If stuck past 15 min, peek at a hint (not the full solution) and finish implementing. |
| Hard | 2 sessions (Day 1 + Day 2) | Day 1: read carefully, dry-run by hand, attempt without forcing a finish. Day 2: implement, debug, commit, write one line on the core insight. |

Hard problems are split across consecutive sessions (sometimes spanning a
week boundary). Total: **48 sessions** → ~10 Easy, ~22 Medium, ~8 Hard
(16 Hard sessions).

## Running a session

- **Easy/Medium:** ~5 min recall the pattern, ~20 min solve with timer on,
  ~5 min commit to `solutions/<n>.go`.
- **Hard:** split across two sessions as in the time-budget table — don't
  compress both days into one.
- Follow the existing `<number>.go` naming convention in `solutions/`.
- After each session: check `[x]` on that row, bump the Progress counts at
  the top, and set **Current focus** to the next unchecked session.
- Once a week, re-solve one earlier problem timed with no notes
  (prefer a Medium you found rough).

## Why this ordering

Topics still build on each other (arrays/hashing before sliding window,
recursion comfort before trees/graphs, 1D DP before 2D), but difficulty
is interleaved every week so you never go weeks without a stretch problem
or weeks without a confidence-building Easy/Medium win. Hard problems
appear from Week 1 onward and keep showing up roughly every 2–3 weeks.
